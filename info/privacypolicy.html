Introduction

We are pleased to welcome you to our website. The protection of your personal data is very important to us. In this privacy policy, we inform you about what data we collect when you visit our website, for what purpose we use it, and on what legal basis this is done.

Personal Data

The processing of personal data is carried out exclusively in accordance with the provisions of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Federal Data Protection Act (BDSG).

Types of Data Processed

  • Master data (e.g., names, addresses)
  • Contact data (e.g., email, phone numbers)
  • Content data (e.g., text entries, photographs, videos)
  • Usage data (e.g., visited web pages, access times)
  • Meta/communication data (e.g., device information, IP addresses)

Initial Contact

When you contact us via email, your information is stored to process your inquiry and in case follow-up questions arise. The legal basis for this processing is Article 6(1)(b) of the GDPR for contractual or pre-contractual communication; otherwise, it is Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR.

When you contact us via email, we store the information you provide (e.g., your email address, name, and inquiry) in order to process and respond to your request. We use the services of Spaceship (Spaceship Inc., 11400 W. Olympic Blvd. Suite 200, Los Angeles, CA 90064, USA) for receiving, sending, and storing our emails. Spaceship provides us with the necessary infrastructure and email accounts. Legal basis: The processing of this data is based on Article 6(1)(b) of the GDPR, provided that your inquiry is related to the performance of a contract or is necessary for the implementation of pre-contractual measures. In all other cases, the processing is based on our legitimate interest in the effective handling of inquiries directed to us (Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR).

Transfer to third countries: Since Spaceship is a U.S. company, data may be transferred to servers in the United States. Spaceship’s parent company (Namecheap, Inc.) is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, which ensures an adequate level of data protection.

Retention period: The data you send to us via email will remain with us until you request its deletion, revoke your consent to its storage, or the purpose for storing the data no longer applies (e.g., after your request has been fully processed). Mandatory legal provisions—in particular tax and commercial law retention periods—remain unaffected.

Web Hosting

We host our website on GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub Inc., 88 Colin P Kelly Jr St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA (hereinafter “GitHub”). When you visit our website, GitHub automatically collects information in so-called server log files, which your browser automatically transmits to GitHub. This includes:

  • The IP address of the accessing computer
  • Date and time of the server request
  • Name and URL of the file accessed
  • Website from which the access originated (referrer URL)
  • Browser used and, if applicable, your computer’s operating system

The processing of this data is necessary for the technical operation, security, and stability of the website. The legal basis for this is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (Legitimate Interest). The data is transferred to the United States. GitHub is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, ensuring an adequate level of data protection. For more information, please see GitHub’s Privacy Policy: https://docs.github.com/de/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement.

Adobe Fonts (Typekit)

This website uses external fonts provided by Adobe Fonts (Adobe Systems Incorporated, 345 Park Avenue, San Jose, CA 95110-2704, USA). When you access a page, your browser loads the required web fonts into your browser cache to display texts and fonts correctly. For this purpose, your browser connects to Adobe servers, whereby Adobe learns that our website was accessed via your IP address. According to Adobe, no cookies are stored.

The integration of Adobe Fonts is based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a uniform and visually appealing presentation of our online offer). If data is transferred to the USA, Adobe relies on EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCC) and the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

Google Web Fonts

This site uses web fonts provided by Google (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) for the uniform display of fonts. When you open a page, your browser loads the required web fonts into your browser cache. For this purpose, your browser must establish a direct connection to Google servers, transferring your IP address to Google.

The use of Google Web Fonts is based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in the uniform presentation of our website). If you do not wish this data transfer, we recommend using a browser extension to block external scripts or opting out via our cookie consent banner (if applicable).

Consent

Retention Period

Personal data is stored only for as long as is necessary to fulfill the respective purpose or as required by a statutory retention obligation. Retention obligations under tax and commercial law are generally 6 or 10 years, depending on the type of document.

Cookies

Our website uses cookies. Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device. We distinguish between technically necessary cookies (Art. 25(2) TTDSG in conjunction with Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and cookies that require prior consent (Section 25(1) TTDSG in conjunction with Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

Disclosure of Data

We do not disclose your personal data to third parties for any purposes other than those listed below. We will only disclose your personal data to third parties if this is necessary for the performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), you have expressly consented (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), or we are obligated to do so based on overriding legitimate interests or legal requirements.

If data is transferred to recipients in third countries (outside the EU/EEA), this is done only on the basis of appropriate safeguards in accordance with Art. 44 et seq. of the GDPR (in particular, EU Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision).

User Rights
