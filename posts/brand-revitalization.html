Hauswirt - Brand Revitalization

During my internship at Designers Trust by Timothy Jacob Jensen, I spearheaded the brand revitalization for the kitchen appliances company Hauswirt. After a comprehensive market and competitor analysis, we developed three distinct design concepts, merging the strongest elements into a unified visual direction. We established the complete brand guidelines - including the logo, typography, color palette, packaging, catalogs, business cards, web, and social media presence.
The project is currently undergoing final refinements.
