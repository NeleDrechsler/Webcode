Paper Jazz
Two binding technics in one book

I crafted this prototype, which combines two different bookbinding technics: The coptic binding (red side of the book) and the french link stitch binding (green side of the book).
Both books are connected through a black carton and wrapped in a green and red colored carton with a trick fold. Additionally, both books are made of two different kinds of paper.
