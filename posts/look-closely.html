LOOK CLOSELY!
Interactive exhibition design

The Parade Rooms at Dresden's Residential Castle were built by August II (1670–1733), prince-elector of Saxony and King of Poland. Collaborating with the Staatliche Kunstsammlungen Dresden, our study course created prototypes to explain the deeper intentions of the objects inside. Our digital prototype "LOOK CLOSELY" explains what the showcased insignias of power in the painting "Herrscherportrait" (portrait of August II) mean and compares them to present-day equivalents.
