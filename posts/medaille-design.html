Medaille Design
Klosterløbet charity run 2025

The annual Klosterløbet charity run takes place in the historic surroundings of Ørslevkloster. Its 2025 proceeds benefited BROEN Danmark to help vulnerable children to join local sports and cultural activities. The Design Studio of Timothy Jacob Jensen was asked to design this year's medal. I had the honor of creating it alongside him. From three concepts, the organization selected a pictogram design capturing the fjord landscape along the running track. The medal is made of 95% upcycled acrylic.
