GUARDIAN - Bachelor thesis
Swim suit for people with chronic diseases

For people living with chronic diseases causing spontaneous fainting, like epilepsy, heart diseases or diabetes mellitus, drowning is the leading cause of death*. This project aims to create a participatory design for swimwear that triggers a rescue system in the event of a medical emergency to prevent the person from drowning. In addition, outsiders should be alerted via a smartwatch application and guided through the rescue procedure.
