Mobile Information Stand
for the Faculty Design

The Dresden University of Applied Sciences (HTWD) organizes an annual open day to give students insight into offered courses. For the faculty design, my information stand was selected in a semester competition and built by the HTWD. Made of MDF panels, the mobile stands can be extended vertically. The aim was to present student projects in product design (left stand; model showcase) and communication design (right stand; interactive and graphic works on the iMac).
