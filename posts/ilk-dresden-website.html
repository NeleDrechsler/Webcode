ILK Dresden Website -
UI/UX and Contentmanagement

During my time as a working student at ILK Dresden (Institut für Luft- und Kältetechnik gGmbH), my key responsibility was spearheading the redesign of the company website.
While MD-Netdesign handled development and Daniel Madlung provided the visual design, my colleague and I drove the core UX decisions—restructuring the navigation hierarchy, mapping user flows, and managing full backend content integration based on the legacy site you can see on the left side.
