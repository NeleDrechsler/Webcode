Book Cover Design -
PhD Thesis in Solid State Physics

I had the favor to design the book cover for my client's solid-state physics PhD dissertation, "Frustration-induced unconventional metals and magnets". The research investigates chiral metals and cobalt niobate, with the latter's vibrant spin-wave excitation spectrum prominently featuring on the cover. Following TU Dresden's corporate identity, the layout incorporates the university logo and Noto Sans typeface. The publication (SLUB, DNB) is available in both softcover and hardcover bindings.
