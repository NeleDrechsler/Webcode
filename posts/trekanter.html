Pencil holder
Trekanter

One of the most important tools for a designer are selected drawing pencils and pens. In order to have them always at hand during the working process, I designed the Trekanter pencil holder.

It's stackable, 3D-printable and recycable. They come in two different versions: one for pencils, and one for pens.
