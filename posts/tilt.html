Desk lamp
TILT°

The TILT° desk lamp lets you “pull” the light toward you: It stands upright in its default position. When the lampshade is pulled toward the user, the lamp shines in the direction of movement. From there, it can be swiveled through a 360° angle. The LED strips adjust their brightness to match, and the light moves with the lampshade's rotation. When the light is no longer needed, the lampshade can be moved back vertically to its original position. Then, the light turns off until moved again.
