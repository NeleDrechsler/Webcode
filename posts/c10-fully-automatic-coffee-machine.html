C10 Fullyautomatic Coffee Machine -
Drafts for Hauswirt

During my internship at Designers Trust, I designed kitchen appliances for Hauswirt, including a collaborative concept for a fully automatic coffee machine alongside Timothy Jacob Jensen. The rendering on the left reflects his initial vision, while the design on the right showcases my own iteration.
The proposed material palette combines polished copper, chrome, and opaque black glass.
