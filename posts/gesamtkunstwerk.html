DELPH-AI
Gesamtkunstwerk as a collective process

For the HfBK "Sommerfest" 2022, TU Dresden computer science and HfBK stage & costume design students merged stage art with AI. A large language model was trained on the Greek methology of Oresteia. The audience typed questions on an old keyboard - the answers got prompted on a huge canvas via a Python text-to-speech app. During the show, students performed a choreography while AI responses were visualized live via VR headsets. The audience watched from a separate lounge with refreshments. Poster Design by dream.ai.
