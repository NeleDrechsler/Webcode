ClipCap - Magnetic swimming goggles-
swim cap combination

Swimmers rarely use swimming caps due to low comfort and the tendency of the goggles to slide off. The aim of the project ClipCap was to solve these issues by giving the cap more functionality through a hybrid material: silicon on the outside for water resistance and polyester lycra blend on the inside to prevent pulling on the hair. The matching swimming goggles are attachable by a magnetic-mechanical mechanism similar to FIDLOCK. For this product, an overall branding was made.
