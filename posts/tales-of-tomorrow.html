Tales of Tomorrow
90th Vocal Night

Students of jazz music and design started a creative collaboration: under the title “Tales of tomorrow”, students of the Hochschule für Musik Dresden (HfMdd) wrote jazz pieces; in addition to that, design students from the University of Applied Science Dresden (HTWD) designed digital visualisations and posters. Presented in the OKA Dresden, the 90th Vocal Night were presented as an overall creative performance. I designed an animated visualisation to kâyames song "4 Walls No Door".
